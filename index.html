DNA Film

DNA Film publishes film story recaps in Indonesian on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, and TikTok, under two brands:

  • DNA Film Asia: films from Asia;
  • DNA Film Global: films from outside Asia.

DNA Film Social (publishing tool)

DNA Film Social is a private command-line tool. The channel owner uses it on their own computer to:

  • upload the channel's own videos and set titles, descriptions, and publish times;
  • read analytics for the channel's own accounts.

The tool is not offered to the public. It acts only on accounts that the owner signs in to and authorizes.
