Privacy Policy — DNA Film Social

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Who uses this tool

DNA Film Social is a private tool. Only the owner of the DNA Film channel uses it, on their own computer, with their own accounts.

Data the tool accesses

After the owner signs in and gives permission, the tool accesses, through the official APIs of YouTube (Google), Instagram, Facebook, Threads (Meta), and TikTok:

  • basic account information (account ID, name);
  • the owner's own videos and posts, and their titles and descriptions;
  • analytics for the owner's own accounts (for example views, likes, watch time).

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos, to set their metadata and publish times, and to show analytics to the owner.

Storage and sharing

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer (macOS Keychain).
  • Analytics are saved as local files on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The tool does not access data of other users.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook / Instagram / Threads: Settings → Apps and Websites (Business Integrations)
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions

Revoking access stops the tool at once. Local copies can be deleted by the owner at any time.
